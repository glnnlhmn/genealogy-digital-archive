# Idea: Individual Evidence Ledger & Research Analysis Tool
<!-- Version: 0.1.0 -->

* **Core Concept:** A comprehensive person dossier tool that extracts an individual's profile while deeply analyzing supporting documents, evidence conflicts, and evidentiary gaps to serve as an actionable research roadmap.
* **Dual Output Modes:**
  * **Machine-Readable Format (JSON/TSV):** Pure relational/structured data designed to feed directly into downstream AI prompts, automation scripts, or spreadsheet ledgers without token-heavy formatting overhead.
  * **Human-Readable Format (Markdown/Report):** Formatted narrative and summary tables designed for quick visual review and reference.
* **Key Report Sections:**
  * **Person Profile:** Canonical name, aliases/spelling variants, vital events, and immediate relational links.
  * **Evidence & Document Ledger:** Chronological matrix of all asserted events paired directly with source citations, document paths, and confidence/quay scores.
  * **Document & Citation Analysis:** Breakdown of how primary/secondary records corroborate, contradict, or leave holes in specific life phases.
  * **Evidentiary Gaps & Timeline Conflicts:** Direct identification of missing decennial censuses, unverified vital records, and conflicting dates/localities across sources.
  * **Research Action Plan:** Concrete next steps, targeted county clerk/archive repositories, and collateral/FAN club leads to pursue.
